About these estimates

Dollar figures in the tooltips are estimates for FY 2026-27, based on each parcel's net taxable value on the 2026 assessment roll. Footprints with several assessor parcels (e.g. condos) are summed.

  • Est. property tax paid = net taxable value × the tax rate area's total secured rate: the 1% general levy plus voter-approved bonds (debt-service rates for schools, the city, water districts, etc.; LA County Auditor-Controller CR12).
  • City revenue (map height) = the part of the 1% levy apportioned to the parcel's city, or to the County general fund where unincorporated (Auditor-Controller AF49).
  • Not included: direct assessments and special taxes billed as separate line items (lighting, sewer, stormwater/Measure W, trauma/Measure B, Mello-Roos, etc.), supplemental and unsecured bills, and penalties. These vary parcel by parcel and aren't published in bulk; an owner's exact bill is on the LA County Treasurer and Tax Collector's site (ttc.lacounty.gov).
  • Exempt parcels (government, schools, nonprofits, etc.) show $0.
  • Mixed-use = a parcel with an assessor combination use code (store or office with residential, artist-in-residence, hotel with apartments), or a footprint whose parcels include both residential and commercial/industrial uses. Residential units count units on residential and mixed-use parcels only.
